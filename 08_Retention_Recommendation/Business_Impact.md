# Business Impact Analysis

## Objective

Estimate the potential business value of implementing the retention recommendation engine.

## Analysis Performed

- Priority Customer Analysis
- Campaign Coverage Analysis
- Customer Value Analysis
- Revenue Protection Estimation
- Executive KPI Summary

## Key Metrics

- Total Customers
- Critical Customers
- Recommendation Types
- Campaign Types
- Estimated Revenue Protected

## Assumptions

The revenue protection estimate assumes that a percentage of high-risk customer revenue can be retained through successful intervention. This is a business scenario for demonstration purposes rather than a measured business outcome.

## Business Value

The analysis demonstrates how predictive analytics can support business decisions by helping prioritize customer retention initiatives and estimate potential financial impact.
