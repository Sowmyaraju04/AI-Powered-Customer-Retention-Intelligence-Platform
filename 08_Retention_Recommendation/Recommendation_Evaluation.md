# Recommendation Evaluation

## Objective

Evaluate the distribution and effectiveness of the generated retention recommendations.

## Analysis Performed

- Recommendation Distribution
- Campaign Type Distribution
- Business Priority Distribution
- Customer Value vs Recommendation Analysis
- Risk Level vs Recommendation Analysis

## Key Outputs

Generated multiple business charts including:

- Recommendation Distribution
- Campaign Distribution
- Priority Distribution
- Customer Value Analysis
- Risk Level Analysis

## Business Value

These analyses help business stakeholders understand how customers are distributed across different retention campaigns, allowing efficient resource planning and campaign prioritization.
