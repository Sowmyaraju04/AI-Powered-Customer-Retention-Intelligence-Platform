# Recommendation Logic

## Objective

Develop a rule-based retention recommendation engine that converts customer risk predictions into actionable business strategies.

## Recommendation Strategy

The recommendation engine evaluates multiple customer attributes, including:

- Risk Level
- Customer Value
- Review Score
- Purchase Frequency

Based on these factors, appropriate retention actions are assigned.

## Example Recommendations

- VIP Service Recovery
- Customer Support Follow-Up
- Exclusive Loyalty Offer
- Personalized Discount
- Re-engagement Email
- Loyalty Reward
- Personalized Promotion
- VIP Appreciation
- Cross-Sell Recommendation

## Business Outputs

Additional business fields generated:

- Recommendation
- Campaign_Type
- Business_Reason
- Expected_Outcome

## Business Value

Instead of simply identifying high-risk customers, the system recommends specific retention strategies, enabling faster and more consistent business decision-making.
