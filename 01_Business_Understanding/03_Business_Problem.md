# Business Problem

## Current Situation

The company currently relies on historical reports to evaluate business performance. Although these reports provide useful insights into sales and customer behavior, they do not identify customers who are likely to churn before it happens.

As a result, retention efforts are often reactive rather than proactive.

---

## Challenges

- No system to identify high-risk customers.
- No estimate of revenue at risk.
- Limited understanding of the drivers behind churn.
- Marketing campaigns are not prioritized based on churn risk.
- Customer success teams lack actionable insights.

---

## Proposed Solution

Develop an AI-powered Customer Retention Intelligence Platform capable of:

- Predicting customer churn.
- Explaining prediction drivers.
- Assigning customer risk scores.
- Estimating revenue at risk.
- Recommending personalized retention strategies.

This solution enables business teams to take proactive actions before customers are lost.
