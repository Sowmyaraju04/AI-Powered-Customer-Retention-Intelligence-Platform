# Industry Overview

## E-Commerce Industry

The e-commerce industry is highly competitive, where customers can easily switch between platforms based on pricing, product availability, delivery experience, and customer service.

As competition increases, retaining existing customers has become more valuable than continuously acquiring new ones.

---

## Importance of Customer Retention

Customer retention is the ability of a business to maintain long-term relationships with existing customers. High retention rates generally lead to:

- Increased customer lifetime value
- Higher repeat purchase rates
- Lower customer acquisition costs
- Improved profitability
- Greater customer loyalty

Businesses therefore invest heavily in customer analytics, loyalty programs, recommendation systems, and predictive models to improve retention.

---

## Why AI is Important

Traditional reporting identifies what has already happened.

Artificial Intelligence enables organizations to predict future customer behavior, identify at-risk customers, and recommend personalized actions before customers are lost.

This shift from reactive reporting to proactive decision-making provides a significant competitive advantage.
