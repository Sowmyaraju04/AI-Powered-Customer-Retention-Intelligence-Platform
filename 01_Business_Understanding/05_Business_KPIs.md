# Business KPIs

The success of the Customer Retention Intelligence Platform will be measured using the following business metrics:

| KPI | Business Importance |
|------|---------------------|
| Customer Churn Rate | Measures customer loss over time. |
| Customer Retention Rate | Measures the ability to retain customers. |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | Indicates customer loyalty. |
| Average Order Value | Measures average customer spending. |
| Customer Lifetime Value (Proxy) | Estimates long-term customer value. |
| Revenue at Risk | Estimates potential revenue loss from high-risk customers. |
| High-Risk Customer Count | Number of customers requiring immediate attention. |

These KPIs will support executive decision-making and performance monitoring.
