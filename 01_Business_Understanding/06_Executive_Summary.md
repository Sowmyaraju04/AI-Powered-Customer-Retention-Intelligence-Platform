# Executive Summary

The AI-Powered Customer Retention Intelligence Platform transforms historical e-commerce data into proactive business intelligence.

By combining customer analytics, machine learning, explainable AI, and business intelligence, the platform identifies customers who are likely to churn, estimates the associated revenue risk, and recommends personalized retention strategies.

The solution supports marketing, CRM, customer success, and executive leadership teams by enabling data-driven decision-making and improving long-term customer profitability.
