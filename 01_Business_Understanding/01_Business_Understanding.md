# Business Understanding

## Project Title

AI-Powered Customer Retention Intelligence Platform

---

## Overview

Customer retention is one of the most important drivers of long-term business success in the e-commerce industry. While acquiring new customers increases market reach, retaining existing customers improves profitability, customer lifetime value, and sustainable revenue growth.

This project aims to build an AI-powered Customer Retention Intelligence Platform that enables organizations to proactively identify customers who are likely to churn, understand the reasons behind the prediction, estimate the potential business impact, and recommend personalized retention strategies.

Unlike traditional reporting systems that focus on historical performance, this platform emphasizes predictive and prescriptive analytics to support proactive business decisions.

---

## Business Context

The Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset contains customer, order, payment, review, seller, and product information. Although these datasets provide rich historical insights, they do not directly answer future-oriented business questions such as:

- Which customers are likely to stop purchasing?
- Why are these customers at risk?
- How much revenue could be lost?
- Which customers should be prioritized for retention?

This project bridges that gap by transforming historical transactional data into an intelligent decision-support system.

---

## Project Vision

The vision of this project is to develop an enterprise-grade AI solution that combines data engineering, business analytics, machine learning, explainable AI, and business intelligence into a single platform. The final solution will help marketing, CRM, customer success, and executive leadership teams make proactive, data-driven decisions to improve customer retention.

---

## Expected Business Outcomes

- Early identification of customers at risk of churn.
- Improved customer retention through proactive engagement.
- Reduced revenue loss associated with customer churn.
- Better allocation of marketing and customer success resources.
- Executive-level visibility into customer health and revenue risk.
