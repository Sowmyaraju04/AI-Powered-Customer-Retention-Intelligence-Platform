# Business Objectives

## Primary Objectives

- Predict customers who are likely to churn.
- Estimate churn probability for every customer.
- Explain the factors influencing churn predictions.
- Prioritize customers based on churn risk and business value.
- Recommend personalized retention strategies.
- Estimate revenue at risk.

---

## Secondary Objectives

- Build a customer-level analytical data mart.
- Develop an explainable machine learning model.
- Create a professional Streamlit application.
- Build an executive Power BI dashboard.
- Produce a recruiter-ready portfolio project.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Develop a scalable AI platform that supports proactive customer retention strategies and executive decision-making.
