# Product Price Analysis

## Business Question

How are product prices distributed across the marketplace?

---

## Observation

The product price distribution is right-skewed, with most products concentrated in the lower price range. A small number of premium-priced products create a long tail and appear as outliers.

---

## Key Insights

- Most products are sold at relatively affordable prices.
- The median price is lower than the mean, indicating a right-skewed distribution.
- A small number of expensive products increase the average selling price.
- Premium products represent niche segments of the marketplace.
- Outliers should be considered carefully during statistical analysis and predictive modeling.

---

## Business Impact

Understanding product price distribution helps optimize pricing strategies, customer segmentation, and revenue forecasting. It also highlights the importance of handling outliers appropriately in future machine learning models.
