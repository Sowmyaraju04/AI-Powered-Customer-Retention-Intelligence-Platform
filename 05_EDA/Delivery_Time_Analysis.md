# Delivery Time Analysis

## Business Question

How efficiently are customer orders delivered?

---

## Observation

Most customer orders are delivered within a reasonable timeframe. Although a small number of deliveries experience delays, overall delivery performance remains satisfactory.

---

## Key Insights

- Average delivery time is within an acceptable range.
- Most orders are delivered on schedule.
- A small number of delayed deliveries create outliers.
- Delivery efficiency contributes positively to customer satisfaction.
- Faster deliveries can further improve customer retention.

---

## Business Impact

Monitoring delivery performance helps improve logistics operations, customer satisfaction, and long-term customer loyalty.
