# Payment Type Analysis

## Business Question

Which payment methods are most preferred by customers when making purchases?

---

## Observation

The payment type distribution shows that credit cards are the most frequently used payment method, followed by boleto, vouchers, and debit cards. This indicates a strong customer preference for card-based transactions.

---

## Key Insights

- Credit cards are the dominant payment method.
- Boleto is the second most commonly used payment option.
- Voucher payments contribute a smaller share of transactions.
- Debit card usage is relatively low.
- Customer payment preferences can help optimize checkout strategies and promotional campaigns.

---

## Business Impact

Understanding payment preferences enables the business to improve the checkout experience, negotiate better payment gateway services, and design targeted offers for the most commonly used payment methods.
