# Review Score Analysis

## Business Question

How satisfied are customers with their shopping experience?

---

## Observation

The review score distribution indicates that the majority of customers provided high ratings, with 5-star reviews representing the largest share. Lower ratings (1 and 2 stars) account for a much smaller proportion of total reviews.

---

## Key Insights

- Most customers are satisfied with their purchases.
- 5-star reviews dominate the dataset.
- Negative reviews (1–2 stars) represent a relatively small percentage.
- Customer satisfaction levels are generally high.
- Review scores can be used as an important feature for customer retention and churn prediction.

---

## Business Impact

High customer satisfaction contributes to stronger customer loyalty and repeat purchases. Customers with low review scores should be monitored, as they may be at higher risk of not returning.
