# Customer State Analysis

## Business Question

Which Brazilian states contribute the largest customer base?

---

## Observation

Customer distribution is concentrated within a few major Brazilian states, while several regions remain underrepresented.

---

## Key Insights

- Most customers originate from a small number of states.
- Regional demand varies significantly across Brazil.
- Major metropolitan areas contribute the highest customer volume.
- Several regions represent potential growth opportunities.
- Geographic analysis supports targeted regional marketing.

---

## Business Impact

Regional customer insights enable businesses to optimize logistics, marketing investments, warehouse locations, and expansion strategies.
