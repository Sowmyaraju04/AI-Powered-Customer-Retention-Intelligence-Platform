# Order Status Analysis

## Business Question

How efficiently does the marketplace fulfill customer orders?

---

## Observation

The distribution of order statuses shows that the vast majority of customer orders were successfully delivered. Only a small percentage of orders were cancelled, unavailable, or remained in intermediate processing stages.

---

## Key Insights

- Most orders reached customers successfully.
- Cancelled orders represent only a small fraction of total orders.
- Processing and shipped orders indicate orders currently moving through the fulfillment pipeline.
- Overall order fulfillment performance is strong.
- The marketplace demonstrates a high operational success rate.

---

## Business Impact

A high delivery success rate increases customer satisfaction and strengthens customer trust. Monitoring cancellation and unavailable orders can help identify operational bottlenecks and opportunities for process improvement.
