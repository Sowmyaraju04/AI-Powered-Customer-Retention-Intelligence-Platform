# Monthly Order Trend Analysis

## Business Question

How has customer order volume changed over time?

---

## Observation

The monthly order trend shows a steady increase in customer purchases over the observed period, indicating consistent marketplace growth. Seasonal fluctuations are visible, but the overall trend reflects increasing customer engagement.

---

## Key Insights

- Monthly order volume shows positive growth over time.
- Customer demand increased consistently across the study period.
- Certain months experienced peak order activity, indicating seasonal purchasing behavior.
- The marketplace demonstrates healthy customer acquisition and retention.
- Growing order volume supports long-term business expansion.

---

## Business Impact

Understanding monthly order trends helps businesses forecast demand, optimize inventory planning, allocate operational resources efficiently, and prepare for seasonal sales periods.
