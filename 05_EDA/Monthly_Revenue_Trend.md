# Monthly Revenue Trend Analysis

## Business Question

How has business revenue changed over time?

---

## Observation

Monthly revenue follows an overall upward trend, closely aligning with the increase in customer orders. This indicates sustainable business growth rather than isolated high-value transactions.

---

## Key Insights

- Revenue increased steadily throughout the analysis period.
- Revenue growth closely follows customer order growth.
- Peak revenue months coincide with higher purchasing activity.
- Business performance demonstrates healthy financial growth.
- Revenue trends support long-term business sustainability.

---

## Business Impact

Revenue trend analysis enables better financial forecasting, budgeting, sales planning, and strategic business decision-making.
