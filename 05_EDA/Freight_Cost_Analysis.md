# Freight Cost Analysis

## Business Question

How are freight charges distributed across customer orders?

---

## Observation

The freight cost distribution indicates that most orders incur relatively low shipping charges, while a small number of orders have significantly higher freight costs. This results in a right-skewed distribution with a few high-cost outliers.

---

## Key Insights

- Most customers pay low to moderate shipping charges.
- Freight costs are concentrated within a narrow range.
- A small number of orders experience exceptionally high shipping costs.
- High freight costs may be influenced by product size, weight, or delivery distance.
- Shipping cost optimization presents an opportunity to improve customer satisfaction.

---

## Business Impact

Managing freight costs effectively can improve the customer experience, reduce cart abandonment, and strengthen customer retention.
