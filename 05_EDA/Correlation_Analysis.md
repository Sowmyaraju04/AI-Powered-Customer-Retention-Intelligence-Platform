# Correlation Analysis

## Business Question

What relationships exist between key numerical variables?

---

## Observation

The correlation analysis indicates meaningful relationships between variables such as product price, payment value, freight cost, and review scores. These relationships provide valuable inputs for predictive modeling.

---

## Key Insights

- Payment value is positively correlated with product price.
- Freight cost generally increases with higher-priced orders.
- Review scores show relatively weak correlation with financial variables.
- Numerical relationships support feature selection for machine learning.
- Correlation analysis helps identify important predictive variables.

---

## Business Impact

Understanding variable relationships improves feature engineering, predictive model development, and business decision-making.
