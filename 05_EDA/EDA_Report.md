# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Report

## Project

**AI-Powered Customer Retention Intelligence Platform**

---

# Objective

The objective of this Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) phase was to understand customer behavior, purchasing patterns, payment preferences, delivery performance, product performance, and operational trends using the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset.

The insights obtained during this phase will support feature engineering, predictive modeling, and customer retention strategies.

---

# Datasets Used

- Customers
- Orders
- Order Items
- Payments
- Products
- Reviews
- Sellers
- Geolocation
- Product Category Translation

---

# Analyses Performed

## 1. Order Status Analysis

- Evaluated order fulfillment performance.
- Identified delivered, cancelled, shipped, and unavailable orders.
- Measured operational efficiency.

---

## 2. Review Score Analysis

- Analyzed customer satisfaction levels.
- Examined review score distribution.
- Identified opportunities for customer retention.

---

## 3. Payment Type Analysis

- Studied customer payment preferences.
- Compared payment methods across all transactions.
- Identified the dominant payment channel.

---

## 4. Product Price Analysis

- Examined product price distribution.
- Identified pricing patterns.
- Detected premium-priced outliers.

---

## 5. Freight Cost Analysis

- Evaluated shipping cost distribution.
- Identified high freight cost orders.
- Assessed logistics efficiency.

---

## 6. Monthly Order Trend

- Measured monthly business growth.
- Identified seasonal purchasing patterns.
- Evaluated customer acquisition trends.

---

## 7. Monthly Revenue Trend

- Tracked revenue growth over time.
- Compared revenue with order volume.
- Evaluated financial performance.

---

## 8. Product Category Analysis

- Identified top-performing product categories.
- Measured customer purchasing preferences.
- Evaluated category-level demand.

---

## 9. Customer State Analysis

- Examined geographical customer distribution.
- Identified major customer markets.
- Highlighted regional expansion opportunities.

---

## 10. Delivery Time Analysis

- Measured average delivery duration.
- Identified delayed deliveries.
- Evaluated logistics performance.

---

## 11. Correlation Analysis

- Examined relationships among numerical variables.
- Identified important variables for predictive modeling.
- Supported feature engineering decisions.

---

# Key Business Findings

- Most orders were successfully delivered.
- Customer satisfaction was generally high.
- Credit cards were the preferred payment method.
- Most products were priced within an affordable range.
- Freight costs remained reasonable for most transactions.
- Monthly orders showed consistent growth.
- Revenue increased steadily over time.
- Product demand was concentrated within a few categories.
- Customer distribution was concentrated in major Brazilian states.
- Delivery performance supported a positive customer experience.
- Numerical relationships identified during correlation analysis will assist future machine learning models.

---

# Business Value

The EDA phase provided valuable insights into customer behavior, operational performance, pricing strategy, logistics efficiency, and business growth.

These findings establish a strong analytical foundation for customer retention modeling and support data-driven business decision-making.

---

# Outcome

The exploratory analysis confirms that the cleaned dataset is well-structured, reliable, and suitable for Feature Engineering, Machine Learning, Explainable AI, and Customer Retention Prediction.
