# Text Standardization

## Objective

Standardize text columns to improve consistency and data quality for analysis and machine learning.

---

## Standardization Rules

- Removed leading and trailing spaces.
- Converted order status to lowercase.
- Converted product category names to lowercase.
- Converted customer city names to lowercase.
- Converted customer state codes to uppercase.

---

## Business Justification

Standardizing text values prevents duplicate categories caused by inconsistent capitalization or spacing. It also improves grouping, filtering, joins, and feature engineering.
