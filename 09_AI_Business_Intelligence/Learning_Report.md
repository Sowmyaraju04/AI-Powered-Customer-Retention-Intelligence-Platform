The AI Business Intelligence layer bridges the gap between predictive analytics and business communication by converting complex machine learning outputs into understandable business insights that support decision-making.

- Preparing structured data for AI systems.
- Generating explainable customer-level insights.
- Developing executive-level business summaries.
- Building AI-assisted business reporting workflows.
- Translating analytics into actionable business intelligence.

This phase demonstrates how AI can be integrated with analytics to enhance business communication and support data-driven customer retention strategies. It completes the transition from predictive analytics to an AI-assisted decision support platform.
