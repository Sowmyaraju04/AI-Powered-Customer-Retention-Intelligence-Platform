# AI Executive Summary

## Objective

Create executive-level business summaries using customer retention insights generated throughout the project.

## Activities Performed

- Loaded customer AI explanations.
- Calculated executive KPIs.
- Generated overall business insights.
- Created summary statistics.
- Produced executive business observations.
- Saved the executive summary dataset.

## Executive KPIs

- Total Customers
- High Risk Customers
- Average Risk Probability
- Average Review Score
- Average Customer Spend
- Recommendation Types
- Campaign Types

## Business Value

The executive summary provides senior management with a concise overview of customer retention performance, helping decision-makers prioritize business initiatives without reviewing detailed customer-level data.
