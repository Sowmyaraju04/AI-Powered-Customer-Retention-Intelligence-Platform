# AI Customer Explanation

## Objective

Generate human-readable customer-level explanations that describe customer risk, business context, and recommended retention actions.

## Activities Performed

- Loaded the AI-ready dataset.
- Generated AI-style customer explanations.
- Included customer value, churn risk, review score, campaign type, and expected business outcome.
- Evaluated explanation length and consistency.
- Saved the enriched dataset.

## Input

- ai_ready_dataset.csv

## Output

- ai_customer_explanations.csv

## Business Value

Business users often require explanations rather than raw model predictions. This notebook converts technical outputs into understandable customer narratives that support customer success teams, marketing teams, and business managers.
