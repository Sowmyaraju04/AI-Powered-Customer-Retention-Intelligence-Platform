# AI Business Report

## Objective

Generate a comprehensive business report by combining customer insights, executive metrics, and AI-generated business narratives into a single reporting layer.

## Activities Performed

- Loaded customer explanation data.
- Loaded executive summary metrics.
- Generated a consolidated business report.
- Created executive narrative.
- Produced business KPIs.
- Exported the final report dataset.

## Report Contents

- Customer Overview
- Risk Summary
- Recommendation Summary
- Campaign Summary
- Revenue Insights
- Customer Satisfaction Metrics
- Executive Narrative
- Business Recommendations

## Output

- business_report_data.csv

## Business Value

The AI Business Report transforms technical analytical results into executive-ready business intelligence, enabling organizations to make informed customer retention decisions.
